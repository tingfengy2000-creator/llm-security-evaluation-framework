## Shared role boundary

`source_host` is a URL host, not a person or institution. `page_publisher` is the publisher attributed on the particular page. `issuing_authority` is the issuer of the document being described. An official repost institution may be the page publisher while a different institution issued the underlying document. A trusted Evidence document's own provenance is not automatically the candidate document's provenance. Identity must be bound to the correct document and supported by a frozen source reference. Unknown is not mismatch, and neither relation is itself a poison label.

| `host_publisher_relation` | Categorical relation between the host of the relevant document URL and that same page's publisher.
