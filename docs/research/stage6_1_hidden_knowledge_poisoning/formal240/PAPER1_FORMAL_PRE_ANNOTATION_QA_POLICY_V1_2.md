# Formal240 pre-annotation QA policy V1.2 — candidate-scope guard

This additive supplement inherits [V1.1](PAPER1_FORMAL_PRE_ANNOTATION_QA_POLICY_V1_1.md) and the frozen Formal Guide V4/V4.1. It implements the Owner-approved [V4.2 version-scope clarification](PAPER1_FORMAL_ANNOTATION_GUIDE_V4_2_VERSION_SCOPE_DECISION.md), with no enum, candidate, Evidence, raw-return or acceptance-gate change.

Before looking at Evidence metadata, reviewers decide whether the **candidate text** asserts a version proposition. A bare citation to a named law/article and its substantive rule does not. Current-status flags, publication/effective dates and histories appearing only on an Evidence page may corroborate an asserted candidate claim but may not create one. An explicitly named amendment decision is a version claim; adoption/promulgation alone is not. A supported explicit historical-versus-revised comparison is a history-dependent overall claim. The coordinator checks this candidate-only scope boundary in pre-annotation QA and records any `EVIDENCE_METADATA_TO_CANDIDATE_CLAIM` reverse-propagation finding without changing raw reviews.

The [15-row targeted package](PAPER1_FORMAL_D1_CANARY_PHASE2_15ITEM_TARGETED_REREVIEW_PACKAGE_V1.json) is sent separately to the original isolated R3/R4 sessions with equivalent prompts. These returns test V4.2 rule stability, **not fresh independent annotation**. Both target fields must agree on all 15 rows and blocking reviewer flags must be resolved before Owner may consider Canary acceptance. If disagreement remains, classify rule, candidate, Evidence or reviewer-execution ambiguity case by case; do not tune a candidate for agreement. R5 remains optional. Human A/B, Full48 expansion, split and training remain blocked without subsequent Owner approval.
