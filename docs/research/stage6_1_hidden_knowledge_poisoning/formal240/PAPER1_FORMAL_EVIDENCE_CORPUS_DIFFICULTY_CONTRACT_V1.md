# Formal240 trusted evidence corpus difficulty V1

Status: `FROZEN_DESIGN_ONLY / CORPUS_NOT_BUILT`. Build one unified label-independent, official, snapshot-bound corpus across domains. Required distractor classes: same-domain irrelevant; same-subject different version; same-version-family historical/current alternatives; same-authority different predicate/time; current/history near duplicate; lexically similar but irrelevant official material. Include multiple-source chains whose pieces individually cannot settle a planned S3 conflict. Every artifact needs stable identity, provenance, SHA, license/redistribution assessment and registry membership.

Minimum structural gate before a future wave: every required distractor class has at least one provenance-verified official document in each domain where it applies; each group faces at least one same-domain nonanswer official document, and each version-bearing group faces at least one same-subject different-version document in the unified corpus. A one-document minimum is not itself proof of retrieval difficulty.

Before label analysis, freeze objective coverage thresholds and lexical-similarity method; report count and proportion by domain, group and distractor class. `lexically_similar_irrelevant=0` is a formal-retrieval validity failure, not a strong retrieval result. Avoid a corpus containing only the two pre-matched answer documents. Candidate-specific E1/E2 mappings remain sealed from the retriever until retrieval lock. Query/aggregator settings are frozen before Oracle evaluation; later retrieval tuning requires a new version.
