# Formal240 pre-GT feature variance and shortcut gate V1

Status: `FROZEN_AUDIT_PLAN / NOT_RUN`. Before future GT freeze, independently audit each candidate-level signal for observed count, applicable/NOT_APPLICABLE/INPUT_MISSING counts, constant and near-constant values, exact duplicate columns, high correlation, scale/outliers, provenance and lawful inference. Segment availability and variance by domain, HKP, group, candidate role and later adjudicated class **only inside the evaluation/control plane**; the detector never receives those labels. Detect class shortcuts from missingness, year tokens, source form, query wording, document age or corpus membership. Inspect fold-local preprocessing feasibility after a future group-aware split; do not fit it now.

Predeclare numerical flags before results: constant = one distinct nonmissing value; near-constant = modal frequency at least 95% among observed values; duplicate = identical value+availability vectors; high absolute Spearman correlation at least 0.95 on at least 30 paired observed rows. These are **review triggers**, not automatic feature deletions. Never remove/redefine a feature because of AUROC, coefficients, S-only comparison or test-set results. A scientific change requires a versioned contract and Owner gate. Preserve raw values and audit report even when a feature is weak.
